Consular Services in Cambodia

Cambodia's consular services are managed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MFAIC) along with embassies and consulates. These services are essential for individuals and businesses dealing with legal documents for international use.

Document Legalization & Authentication

A. Foreign Documents for Use in Cambodia

  1. Notarization in Home Country: Documents must be notarized by a recognized authority.
  2. Legalization by Cambodian Embassy: Submit to the nearest Cambodian Embassy/Consulate.
  3. Translation: Some documents require certified Khmer translation.
  4. MFAIC Authentication: Final authentication by Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

B. Cambodian Documents for Use Abroad

  1. Notarization in Cambodia: Certify documents with relevant ministries.
  2. MFAIC Authentication: Ministry of Foreign Affairs must authenticate.
  3. Embassy Legalization: Legalization by the destination country's embassy.
  4. Apostille Note: Cambodia doesn't use apostilles; full legalization is required.

Important Notes

• Processing typically takes 2-5 working days

• Fees vary by document type and processing speed

• Cambodia is not part of the Hague Apostille Convention

Common Documents Requiring Legalization

  • Birth certificates
  • Marriage certificates
  • Divorce decrees
  • Police clearance certificates
  • Academic diplomas
  • Company registrations
  • Powers of attorney
  • Commercial contracts
  • Court rulings
  • Affidavits

Other Consular Services

  • Visa application assistance
  • Cambodian passport services
  • Notary services for signatures
  • Assistance to Cambodians abroad
