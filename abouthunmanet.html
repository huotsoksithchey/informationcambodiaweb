Hun Manet in 2025

As Cambodia's Prime Minister in 2025, Hun Manet continues to lead the country with a focus on modernization and economic development. The son of former Prime Minister Hun Sen, he represents a new generation of Cambodian leadership.

Key Achievements in 2025

  • Economic Growth: Maintained GDP growth at 6.5% despite global challenges
  • Digital Cambodia: Expanded nationwide 5G coverage and e-government services
  • Infrastructure: Completed Phase 2 of the Phnom Penh-Sihanoukville Expressway
  • Foreign Policy: Strengthened ASEAN relations while balancing ties with China and Western nations
  • Education Reform: Implemented new vocational training programs nationwide

Challenges Faced

In 2025, Prime Minister Hun Manet faces several challenges including:

  • Managing Cambodia's debt-to-GDP ratio
  • Addressing youth unemployment
  • Balancing environmental concerns with development needs
  • Navigating complex international geopolitics

Future Outlook

Looking ahead, the Hun Manet administration plans to focus on:

  • Further digital transformation of government services
  • Expanding Cambodia's manufacturing capabilities
  • Developing renewable energy infrastructure
  • Enhancing regional cooperation within ASEAN

  • កំណើនសេដ្ឋកិច្ច៖ រក្សាកំណើន GDP នៅ 6.5% ទោះបីមានការលំបាកជាសកល

  • ហេដ្ឋារចនាសម្ព័ន្ធ៖ បានបញ្ចប់ដំណាក់កាលទី២នៃផ្លូវយោធាភ្នំពេញ-ព្រះសីហនុ
  • នយោបាយការបរទេស៖ ពង្រឹងទំនាក់ទំនងអាស៊ាន ខណៈដែលរក្សាតុល្យភាពទំនាក់ទំនងជាមួយចិន និងប្រទេសខាងលិច
  • កំណែទម្រង់អប់រំ៖ អនុវត្តកម្មវិធីបណ្តុះបណ្តាលវិជ្ជាជីវថ្មីទូទាំងប្រទេស
