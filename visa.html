Visa Application Fee Updates

Effective May 30, 2023

The application fee for visitor visas for business or tourism (B1/B2s and BCCs), and other non-petition based NIVs such as student and exchange visitor visas, will increase from $160 to $185.

• Petition-based nonimmigrant visas for temporary workers (H, L, O, P, Q, and R categories) will increase from $190 to $205.
• Treaty trader, treaty investor, and treaty applicants in a specialty occupation (E category) will increase from $205 to $315.

We recognize the critical role international travel plays in the U.S. economy. Visa fees are calculated based on the results of an annual study of the cost of service.

Additional Information:

• The Final Rule implementing these NIV fee increases was published in the Federal Register on March 28, 2023: View details

• More information available at: U.S. Visa Information

MRV Fee Validity:

All nonimmigrant visa application fee (MRV fee) payments made on or after October 1, 2022, are valid for 365 days from the payment receipt date. Applicants must schedule an interview or submit a waiver application within this period.

Note: The interview itself doesn't need to occur within 365 days, only the scheduling/submission.

All MRV fee receipts issued before October 1, 2022, were extended until September 30, 2023.

Visa Services in Cambodia:

The United States Embassy Phnom Penh continues to provide:

• All immigrant visa services
• All non-immigrant visa services for Cambodian and resident third country nationals

Since November 01, 2021, limited appointment availability resumed for non-resident third country nationals applying for: B1/B2, F, M and J visas.

Note: Applicants from outside Cambodia may apply, but language barriers and unfamiliarity with local conditions may make demonstrating visa qualifications more challenging.
