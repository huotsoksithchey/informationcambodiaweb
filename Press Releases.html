Press Releases

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Kingdom of Cambodia regularly issues official press releases to disseminate critical updates, policy announcements, and diplomatic statements. These documents serve as the primary source of verified information regarding Cambodia's international relations, bilateral agreements, and multilateral engagements.

Timely Updates: Immediate notifications on diplomatic developments, state visits, and international collaborations.
Policy Clarifications: Official interpretations of Cambodia's foreign policy stances and legislative changes.
Event Announcements: Details on high-level summits, cultural exchanges, and humanitarian initiatives.
Multilingual Accessibility: Releases are published in Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ) and English to ensure broad accessibility.

How to Stay Informed:

Website Portal: Visit the Ministry's official website for the latest statements.
Social Media: Follow verified accounts on platforms like Facebook and Twitter.
Email Subscriptions: Opt-in for direct notifications via the Ministry's mailing list.
